Skulldragon species created by [**CamuuShamuu**](https://furaffinity.net/user/CamuuShamuu)

I thought I'd make a skulldragon variant of Jesper since he already has a skull for a mask anyways, and I thought it'd be funny to see what he'd look like if he put on the wrong mask waking up in the morning.

Since he's got heterochromia in his eyes, I decided to make it so his magic aura stuff is like the two colors blended. So that means he's orange!

Software Used: [Clip Studio Paint](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/purchase/) EX

Date Completed: August 8, 2024
